Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Obol collects no data. It has no accounts, servers, analytics or ads, and it makes no internet connection with your data.

Who is responsible

Obol is an app by Alejandro Tejedo Dura. Contact: alejandro.tejedo@icloud.com.

What is stored, and where

Everything you create in Obol is stored only on your iPhone:

  • your modes, schedules and settings;
  • your study decks and cards;
  • your progress: streak, tolls paid and openings avoided.

None of it is sent to the developer or to third parties. If you back up your iPhone (to iCloud or a computer), that data may be included in your backup, which Apple manages under your account.

Screen Time

To block apps, Obol uses Apple's Screen Time with your permission. The apps, categories and websites you choose are stored as opaque identifiers provided by iOS: Obol cannot tell which apps you have installed or read your usage or browsing history, and that information never leaves your iPhone.

Notifications

If you allow them, notifications (for example, the reminder before the free trial ends) are scheduled on the iPhone itself. No notification servers are used.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Obol only learns from Apple whether Pro is active; it does not receive your name, email or payment details. Apple's handling is governed by its privacy policy.

External links

Links to this page, to help and to the terms of use open in your browser. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data about your visit (such as your IP address) under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you write to me

If you send a support email, I use your address and what you tell me only to reply, and you can ask me to delete the conversation.

Children

Obol is a self-control tool for personal use and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Since the developer holds none of your data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on any server. To remove the data from your iPhone, delete the app. For any question about your data protection rights, write to alejandro.tejedo@icloud.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, it will be published here with a new date. If Obol ever started collecting data, the app would tell you first.
