Obol Help

Obol blocks the apps that distract you and asks for a toll before they open: breathe, wait, or answer one of your own cards.

Contact

Something not working, or have an idea? Write to alejandro.tejedo@icloud.com. I reply personally, usually within one or two working days. For a bug, your iPhone model and iOS version help a lot.

Obol says it has no permission

Obol uses Apple's Screen Time. Open Settings > Screen Time and check that it is on; then, in Settings > Apps > Obol, turn on “Screen Time”. Go back to Obol and tap the button to grant access.

An app is not being blocked

  • Check that the app is in the mode and that the mode is blocking right now (Today tab).
  • iOS can only block 50 individual apps at once. If you pick more, use whole categories: each counts as one.
  • Restart your iPhone if you have just granted access and the block does not appear.

Tapping “Pay toll” does not open Obol

On iOS 26.5 or later, the button opens Obol directly. On iOS 26.0 to 26.4, Obol sends you a notification: tap it to pay the toll. Notifications for Obol must be allowed in Settings > Notifications.

I need the block off right now

You can always end a block early. In Today, choose to end it, type the phrase you are asked for and wait a few seconds. That day does not count towards your streak.

I can't delete Obol or turn off strict mode

With strict mode on, iOS does not allow deleting apps while a block is running, and strict mode cannot be switched off until the block ends. Once it ends (or if you end it early), you can turn it off in Obol's Settings.

Purchases and subscription

  • Restore: in Obol, Settings > Restore Purchases, with the same Apple Account you bought with.
  • Cancel or change plan: iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Obol. If you cancel the free trial at least a day before it ends, you are not charged.
  • Refunds: Apple handles them at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What does the free plan include?

One mode with up to 3 apps or categories and one schedule, all three tolls, one deck of up to 10 cards, the emergency exit and 7 days of progress. Obol Pro removes those limits.

My data

Obol has no accounts and no servers: everything stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
